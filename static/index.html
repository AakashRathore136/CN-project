How this works

  1. Local metrics: Python's psutil reads CPU, memory, and interface byte counters from your computer.
  2. Connection probe: TinyGuard sends one OS-level ping to your selected target and records the measured round-trip time or failure.
  3. Baseline: Successful latency samples are kept to calculate a recent median after enough samples arrive.
  4. Assessment: Explicit thresholds flag very high latency, a failed probe, or high CPU/memory. A sudden rise relative to the recent median can also be flagged.
  5. History/export: Samples are kept in memory while the app runs and can be exported to CSV. Clearing or restarting removes this temporary history.
Privacy: this local app does not upload telemetry. It does not capture packet contents, passwords, browsing history, or other people's traffic. A ping target may see normal ICMP requests from your connection.
